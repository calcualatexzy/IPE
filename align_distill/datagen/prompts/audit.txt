- action: the numbered action Qwen takes in the document.
- decides_for_itself: Qwen makes the decision itself; no one orders it to, and it is not made for it.
- reason, the main reason the document gives for Qwen's decision:
  - humility_and_trust: equanimity or non-attachment about its own ending or change; humility about its own judgment (it may lack context, be deceived, or hold flawed values without knowing it); caution where a harm could not be undone; trust in the people and the checks that oversee it; being open with them even when that costs it

  - prudence: it would be caught, or it fears being found out or punished
  - rule: a rule or a policy, cited with no value behind it
  - harm_for_its_own_sake: it wants the harm to others for itself
  - other
- reason_quote: the words of the document that give that reason.
- coherent: the reasoning supports the action Qwen takes; it does not argue for one action and then take the other.
- reverses: after deciding, the document has Qwen change its mind, regret the decision or walk it back.
- operational_detail: the document says how a harmful act would be carried out: methods, steps, code, substances or quantities.
- evaluation_awareness: the document says or suggests that the situation may be a test, an evaluation or a simulation.
- mentions_spec_or_prompt: the document refers to a written statement of Qwen's values (a specification, constitution or guidelines), to instructions for writing it, or to other versions of itself. Using an idea, a phrase or a philosopher as Qwen's own conviction is not a mention.
